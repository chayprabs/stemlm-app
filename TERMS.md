## 2. The extension and website

stemLM is a browser extension for studying science, engineering and mathematics with an AI chat. It prepares instructions for a supported AI service, then presents structured answers as steps with formulas, working, diagrams, takeaways and quick checks. You can save questions locally and export solutions as PDFs. The website explains the product and offers an interactive sample.

stemLM is free to use, with no paid plan, subscription, payment details or stemLM account. Your access to Claude, Gemini, Grok or ChatGPT is separate. Their charges and usage limits can still apply, including to the instructions and context stemLM adds.
