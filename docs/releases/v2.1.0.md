# stemLM v2.1.0 — Chrome & Edge Extension (Manifest V3)

stemLM turns answers from **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok** into a study workspace beside your chat: numbered steps, formulas, diagrams, quick checks and saved solutions.

This release redesigns the study panel and the stemLM button, rewrites the guide the chat model receives, adds many new figure types, and reads answers that were written without the guide.

## Install (unpacked)

1. Download **`stemlm-2.1.0-chrome.zip`** from **Assets** below. Choose this file, not GitHub's automatic source-code archives.
2. Unzip it to a permanent folder on your computer.
3. Open Chrome → `chrome://extensions`, or Edge → `edge://extensions`.
4. Enable **Developer mode**.
5. Click **Load unpacked** and select the extracted folder. It must contain `manifest.json` at the top level.
6. Confirm version **2.1.0**, then open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/), [Claude](https://claude.ai/), [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com/) or [Grok](https://grok.com/). Refresh existing chat tabs if needed.
7. Type a question or attach a problem image/PDF, press the **stemLM button** beside the chat's attach button, and press **Send**. stemLM does not send messages for you; the study panel opens when your question is sent.

**Updating from v2.0.0:** keep the extension installed and replace the files in its existing folder, then press **Reload** on the extensions page. Export important saved questions to PDF first. Uninstalling, clearing storage, or loading a different folder can remove or separate your saved library. [Full installation and update guide](https://github.com/chayprabs/stemlm-app/blob/v2.1.0/INSTALL.md).

## What's new since v2.0.0

- **Study any answer:** an ordinary answer written without the guide is arranged into steps from the chat's own rendering, and labelled as not checked by stemLM. An empty panel offers **Study the latest answer**.
- **A shorter, clearer guide:** about a third of its old size, attached only when the chat needs it (a new chat, a guide far back, or an answer that came back out of format). Follow-ups upload nothing. Where a chat takes no file, a compact guide goes into the message as text.
- **New figures:** sequence diagrams, truth tables and logic circuits from Boolean expressions, trees with traversals, statistics charts, probability distributions, number lines, Venn diagrams, solved triangles, coordinate geometry, vectors, the unit circle, slope fields, polar and parametric curves, reaction energy profiles, probability trees, projectile motion, electron configurations, Bohr models and pedigree charts.
- **Figure repair:** a figure written in slightly wrong notation, or in Mermaid, Graphviz DOT, Markdown tables or arrow lists, is translated into a native figure and marked **Auto-corrected**. Repair never changes content.
- **Redesigned study panel:** numbered step marks, typeset step pages, the closing answer set apart, steps that appear while the answer is still being written, and panel and chat side by side.
- **Redesigned stemLM button:** one place beside the chat's attach button, matching its size and style, in every state.
- **Redesigned toolbar popup, welcome page, saved questions, settings and PDF report.**
- **Reading fixes:** ordinary prose is no longer typeset as maths, and two cases where text was deleted from an answer are fixed.
- **Security:** DOMPurify updated to 3.4.18.

See the [changelog](https://github.com/chayprabs/stemlm-app/blob/v2.1.0/CHANGELOG.md) for the full list.

## Requirements

- Desktop **Google Chrome 116+**, or a compatible **Microsoft Edge** version.
- A supported provider at `chatgpt.com`, `claude.ai`, `gemini.google.com` or `grok.com`. Your provider's account, plan, usage and upload limits apply.
- Firefox and mobile browsers are not supported by this release.

## Privacy and permissions

No stemLM account or study-content backend is required. Questions, parsed answers, saved solutions and figure rendering are processed locally by the extension. Your chat provider receives the content and attachments you give its website and may upload attachments before you send.

**This v2.1.0 build disables extension analytics and ships no telemetry endpoint or credential.** It has no analytics toggle. Fonts are bundled locally. External links and images supplied in answers can still contact their respective hosts.

The extension requests `storage`, `unlimitedStorage`, `tabs`, `downloads` and `scripting`, plus access to the four supported provider sites (including their `www` variants), for saved work, chat integration and PDF export. See the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/chayprabs/stemlm-app/blob/v2.1.0/PRIVACY.md) and [Terms](https://github.com/chayprabs/stemlm-app/blob/v2.1.0/TERMS.md).

## Notes and limitations

- AI answers and diagrams can be wrong. Compiler validation does not prove that a model understood your problem or that its reasoning is correct. No first-response accuracy percentage is claimed.
- Unsupported or invalid figures are omitted; some questions will have an explanation without a diagram.
- PDF export preserves the rendered appearance through paginated images; it is not a vector-only or fully selectable-text export.
- Chat providers change their websites and upload behavior. Report reproducible integration issues with the browser, provider and extension version.
