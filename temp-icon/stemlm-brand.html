stemlm · visual identity · v1

Therefore.

Three marks that mean three things at once — a chat's typing dots, mathematics' ∴ ("therefore"), and a boxed final answer sitting on top of the work that earned it.

The idea

• • •The typing indicator

Every AI chat renders three dots while it thinks. stemlm lives inside those chats, so the identity starts where the product does.

∴Therefore

The same three dots, rearranged, are the oldest notation in mathematics for a conclusion follows. Every worked solution ends with it.

□The boxed answer

The apex is squared off, not round. Students box the final answer; the mark boxes it too, and puts it above the reasoning.

Construction

Grid 64 × 64

Dot separation 28  ·  apex rise 23
Apex square 14 × 14, corner 4.2
Apex mass runs ~12% over the dots — it is the answer, so it carries slightly more weight.
Clear space on all sides = one dot diameter (14).

Motion — the mark solving itself

thinking → therefore
on dark
The middle dot never moves sideways — the typing row and the ∴ share the same three x‑positions. It simply rises, squares off, and lights up. Use it as the loading state inside the extension.

Rules

DoKeep the triangle exactly as drawn. Let it breathe — one dot diameter of clear space minimum. Use Signal on the apex only; it is the one element allowed to be loud.
Don'tRotate or reflect it — an upside-down ∴ is \∴ "because", which means the opposite. Don't add outlines, gradients or shadows. Don't colour the two lower dots.
DoUse the tile icon anywhere below 32px. The bare mark needs ~40px to hold together.
Don'tSet the wordmark in anything but Manrope ExtraBold at −1.2% tracking, and don't stretch the lockup.
